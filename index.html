FAQ

How often are governance reviews delivered?

A complete governance package is delivered monthly, with quarterly committee deep dives and event-driven alerts within one business day.

What liquidity windows are available?

Standard windows are monthly with advance notice; tailored windows can be engineered at mandate inception to match treasury calendars.

How are drawdown rules enforced?

Hard exposure brakes and variance caps are codified pre-trade; breach protocols trigger immediate de-risking and committee notification.

What does the reporting package include?

You receive attribution layers, allocation drift analysis, scenario notes, and control logs formatted for investment committees and auditors.

Which mandate profiles fit best?

The framework suits institutions seeking measured upside from AI value chains while keeping policy-level risk limits intact.

How is risk containment implemented?

Containment blends corridor sizing, sleeve diversification, and active stress monitoring with documented intervention thresholds.

What is the implementation timeline?

Mandate setup typically takes two to four weeks, including due diligence, control mapping, and reporting alignment.

Which due diligence materials are shared?

Data room access includes governance manuals, methodology notes, operating playbooks, and anonymized historical committee packs.
